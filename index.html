About

I'm Abinash Basa, based in Baripada, Odisha, India. I'm pursuing an M.Sc. in Mathematics at Lovely Professional University and an MBA in Generative AI and Product Management at IIT Patna, building a foundation that sits squarely at the intersection of rigorous thinking and real-world product building.

My interests converge around AI, mathematics, and the craft of building things that work. I think the best product managers are translators, people who can move fluently between technical depth and user needs. That's what I'm training to become.

I write daily on Substack, sharing what I'm learning about AI, math, and building in public. I believe in learning out loud and showing the work, not just the results.
