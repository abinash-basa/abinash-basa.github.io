About Me

Exploring the synergy between analytical mathematics, artificial intelligence, and strategic product execution.

Translating Complexity Into Value

I am based in Baripada, Odisha, India. My academic and professional journey is shaped by a central belief: the most impactful modern product managers are translators who operate fluently across technical depth, mathematical reasoning, and human user experience.

Currently, I am pursuing an MBA in Product Management & Generative AI at IIT Patna (2026–2028), having completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics at Lovely Professional University (2024–2026) and my B.Sc. in Mathematics from MPC Autonomous College (2021–2024).

Through my work at Basa Academies Universal Private Limited, I direct business operations, institutional partnerships, and digital learning programs—leading initiatives that include a 5-school government pilot and AI productivity workshops for over 50 government officials at a District Collector's Office.

I believe in learning out loud. Every single day, I write on Substack about mathematics, AI, and building in public—breaking down complex concepts and sharing honest reflections from real product work.
