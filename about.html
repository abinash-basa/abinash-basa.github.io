Background and What I Do

I am based in Baripada, Odisha, India. My academic background is in mathematics, which gave me a habit of thinking structurally, breaking problems into logical pieces, and working comfortably with data.

Right now, I am pursuing an MBA in Product Management and Generative AI at IIT Patna (2026 - 2028). Before this, I completed an M.Sc. in Mathematics from Lovely Professional University (2024 - 2026) and a B.Sc. in Mathematics from MPC Autonomous College (2021 - 2024).

I am interested in product management, data analytics, and applied AI roles where I can combine quantitative problem-solving with practical execution.

Building BASA Academies

Alongside my academic and technical work, I run BASA Academies (Basa Academies Universal Private Limited), an education and digital transformation business based in Baripada, Odisha.

The venture operates across two connected areas:

  • Education and Training: Providing practical training in digital skills, computer education, programming, AI fundamentals, robotics, and career-oriented learning for students and community cohorts.
  • Digital Transformation: Helping local organizations and schools adopt modern digital tools, implement technology-enabled workflows, and train staff to use software effectively.

I am personally involved in building and running the venture, from structuring training programs and coordinating institutional initiatives to developing our digital systems. Our work has included an AI and digital literacy pilot across 5 government schools, an AI productivity workshop for 50+ officials at the District Collector's Office, and digital skills sessions for 30+ Self-Help Group (SHG) members.
