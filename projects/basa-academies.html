Overview and Context

Basa Academies Universal Private Limited is an educational initiative based in Odisha, focused on providing accessible technical training in AI tools, robotics, programming, and foundational digital literacy.

As Business Operations and Program Lead, I manage training operations, coordinate with school and government partners, structure course delivery, and build the digital systems that support our programs.

Key Programs and Reach

Our programs work with academic institutions, district administration offices, and local community groups:

  • Government School Pilot: Coordinated and delivered an AI, robotics, and digital literacy training program across 5 schools.
  • District Collector's Office Workshop: Structured and delivered a hands-on AI productivity and digital tools workshop for 50+ administrative officials.
  • Self-Help Group (SHG) Sessions: Conducted digital literacy and basic digital financial tools sessions for 30+ SHG members.
  • Student Workshops: Managed scheduling, curriculum pacing, and feedback loops for student cohorts.

Digital Systems and Platform

To support our training programs, I designed and built the organization's multi-page website and inbound inquiry workflows.

System Features

Structured course catalogs, lead capture forms, responsive program schedules, and fast static delivery optimized for mobile devices on regional network connections.

Operations and Stakeholder Management

Running programs across multiple stakeholder groups requires clear planning between instructors, school heads, and district administration coordinators.

Standardizing schedules, attendance tracking, and feedback collection helped make each workshop run predictably and build credibility with institutional partners.

Learnings and Takeaways

  • Audience-Specific Delivery: Government officials need practical productivity workflows, while school students need engaging hands-on projects. Matching the content to the audience is essential.
  • Operational Simplicity: Clean scheduling and direct communication save more time than complicated software setups.
  • Practical Utility: Focusing on everyday tasks makes technology training immediately useful for participants.
