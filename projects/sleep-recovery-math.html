Mathematical Modeling of Sleep Quality and Recovery Rate Dynamics

Formulating and solving differential equation systems to model cognitive restoration and sleep debt recovery trajectories across varying physiological parameters.

Domain Applied Mathematics and Biomathematics

Abstract and Problem Context

Sleep debt recovery is not a linear function of elapsed sleep time. Instead, the rate of restorative biochemical and cognitive restoration exhibits non-linear saturation characteristics dependent on cumulative sleep deficit, circadian phase, and recovery rate constants.

Written during my 3rd semester of M.Sc. Mathematics at Lovely Professional University, this research formulates a mathematical framework modeling the rate of sleep recovery over time.

Mathematical Formulation and ODE Models

The core dynamics were modeled using first-order differential equations representing the rate of change of cognitive deficit D(t) with respect to continuous recovery time t:

Exponential Decay Model

dD/dt = -λ D(t) + S(t)
Where λ denotes the recovery rate coefficient and S(t) captures circadian stimulus effects. Analytical solutions demonstrate exponential decay of sleep debt during continuous sleep bouts.

Numerical Simulation and Euler's Method

To evaluate trajectories under discrete, fragmented sleep schedules, the differential system was solved numerically using Euler's method across multiple parameter variations.

The simulations illustrate how fragmented sleep schedules introduce cumulative recovery friction compared to continuous uninterrupted restoration windows of identical net duration.

Key Insights and Applications

  • Non-Linear Restoration: Early sleep cycles achieve the highest rate of cognitive debt clearance per unit time.
  • Mathematical Grounding: Demonstrates how formal differential equations bridge abstract mathematical theory and human biological systems.
